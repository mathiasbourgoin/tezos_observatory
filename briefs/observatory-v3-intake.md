# Intake Brief — observatory-v3

**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Status: VALIDATED**
**Type:** feature
**Trust boundary:** yes

## Goal

After accepted foundations gates, ASTRA directly implements a fresh, professional and practical Tezos consensus observatory. The result replaces the generic paired-network card composition with a full-width selected-instance workflow: identify the observation, navigate the branching consensus path, inspect exact phase evidence and participants, and keep reading while collection continues. Research and two structurally different studies precede the accepted design/specification/plan.

The timeline follows the maintainer's model: previous block left, observed N/R center, conditional N/R+1 above and N+1/R0 right. The camera moves UP on actual same-level round advancement and RIGHT on actual next-level advancement. Real observed block hashes, attributable candidates, distinct dashed predictions and solid observations, both coherent RPC proposer/timer branches, independent pin/follow-live/collection pause, bounded retention and accessible phone-to-ultrawide interaction are mandatory. A timer cannot manufacture an observation. In particular, when the applied head is at a high round, successor votes remain at observed round 0 even if the clock later estimates rounds 6→7: no camera movement or invented observed rounds 1–6 is permitted.

## Scope Boundary

Out of scope:

- Publication/deployment without separate maintainer approval; server/background collectors, signing, wallets, node mutation and undisclosed provider aggregation.
- Changing accepted foundation identity, accounting, provenance, cancellation, comparison and retention contracts to simplify the visual implementation.
- Claims of infinite retained history, global completeness, network-wide delegate inactivity or unverified finality.
- Receipt timelines, watched filters, recovery celebration and expanded-path mode. These optional features are excluded by the accepted coordinator decision; the required branching navigator is in scope.
- Copying external reference graphics or introducing decorative polling/motion. Native SVG/CSS data graphics are preferred; the reviewed Observatory mark remains.
