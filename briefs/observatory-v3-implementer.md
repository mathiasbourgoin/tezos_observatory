## Specification policy decisions — root, 14 October 2026

They augment this validated intake so a fresh planner receives the complete accepted input, rather than relying on conversation history. They do not claim implementation or production QA.

2. **Latest observed** selects and centers the newest coherent LIVE-eligible observation by first accepted receipt as a one-off inspection/pin action. **Follow live** explicitly enables ongoing eligible selection/camera following. Startup defaults to Follow live. History/candidate/Previous/Latest inspection stops automatic camera following, never collection. Collection pause/resume remains independent. Incoming updates cannot move pinned camera, document scroll or focus. Explicit reader navigation may recenter instantly or briefly/neutrally without claiming progression.
4. A pin freezes opportunity deadline/remaining duration with actual capture time, source/context and historical assumptions.
