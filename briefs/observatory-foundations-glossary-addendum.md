## Core source and context terms

- **Observation source**: the configured endpoint that actually supplied a fact, retained with its immutable receipt. A provider’s name, configured role or URL does not by itself prove a network’s chain identity. Original unpinned receipts keep unknown requested heads unknown. Authority requires actual context proof, independently of whether another configured primary endpoint is available. Sources: [FR013/025/027/076](../specs/observatory-foundations.md), [context collection](../src/rpc/networkData.ts), [admission](../src/monitor/observationStore.ts).
- **Helper responder**: the endpoint that actually returned rights, constants or eligibility evidence. Its original receipt remains distinct from the observer used to bind the selected instance. Existing primary fallback can use a different configured helper only after exact chain/head/parent/producing and execution protocol/target compatibility; its facts are never relabelled as the observer’s or comparison source’s response. Sources: [FR013/030/076/090](../specs/observatory-foundations.md), [targetRequest](../src/rpc/networkData.ts), [comparison authority](../src/consensus/phaseEvidence.ts).
- **Producing protocol**: the full protocol hash returned by pinned RPC protocol metadata for an actually verified block context. **Target execution protocol**: the full protocol hash justified for the target level from its proven anchor’s next-protocol metadata and concrete observer/parent relationship. These are separate from the shell header’s numeric `proto` **protocol-level counter**, which does not identify a protocol hash. Unknown or contradictory protocol facts cannot be inferred from labels or the counter. Sources: [FR025/029/030](../specs/observatory-foundations.md), [RpcContext and TargetContext](../src/rpc/networkData.ts), [raw receipt types](../src/rpc/models.ts).
- **Operation branch**: the operation’s original RPC branch hash, retained as a raw anchor fact. It is not automatically the immediate proposal parent, and inequality with the current head alone does not prove that a vote is invalid. Sources: [FR013/025/029](../specs/observatory-foundations.md), [MonitoredOperation](../src/rpc/models.ts), [guards](../src/rpc/guards.ts).
- **Contextual admission**: a later proof sidecar binding retained original observations to their own configured observer, proven chain/head/parent/target/execution context, rights and lifecycle generation. It can add numerical authority without rewriting the raw receipt or refreshing its first arrival. Context invalidation withdraws LIVE authority immediately while retained original facts remain inspectable. Sources: [FR025–036/048/050](../specs/observatory-foundations.md), [ObservationStore](../src/monitor/observationStore.ts), [ObserverReconciliation](../src/monitor/observerReconciliation.ts).
- **Retained enrichment**: later immutable accepted facts for the same retained historical instance may update its captured inspection without changing the selected identity, reader controls, first receipt, eviction order or newer-item baseline. Eviction from the 100-active-candidate index alone does not end enrichment; eviction from the 150-instance historical owner freezes its separately retained pin. Unknown historical evidence is not filled from unrelated current caches. Sources: [FR049–054/091/093](../specs/observatory-foundations.md), [captureHistory and captureOpportunities](../src/monitor/observationStore.ts).
- **Phase-specific threshold**: the exact quorum requirement belonging to that phase’s accepted numerical basis. Current and captured-maximum committees/thresholds can differ after a rights correction; a preattestation’s retained threshold cannot be replaced by the last rendered attestation’s threshold. Sources: [FR004/011/050/079](../specs/observatory-foundations.md), [AcceptedPhase](../src/monitor/observationStore.ts), [VoteView and gauge rendering](../src/dashboardView.ts), [S9 numeric regression](./observatory-foundations-slice-9-impl.md).

- **Renderable capture/receipt date**: a safe integer millisecond date within the inclusive renderable calendar range. Numerical safety alone does not make a date displayable. Invalid capture or displayed original proposer/timing receipt dates are rejected before retained opportunity publication, without repairing original receipts. The raw applied-header timestamp still follows its separate strict calendar/timezone string guard. Sources: [FR012/013/087/088](../specs/observatory-foundations.md), [shared timing calendar bound](../src/consensus/roundSchedule.ts), [capture boundary](../src/consensus/opportunityFacts.ts), [independent S9 temporal corrections](./observatory-foundations-slice-9-review-fixes.md).

## Conditional opportunities and progression

- **Conditional opportunity**: one of the two branches relative to the selected observed N/R identity: same-level N/R+1 conditional on continued same-level production, or next-level N+1/R0 conditional on hypothetical production. They may coexist. A separate estimated clock round never replaces the selected observed anchor or creates a block/history event. Sources: [FR082–085](../specs/observatory-foundations.md), [OpportunityFact](../src/consensus/roundSchedule.ts), [selection wiring](../src/main.ts).
- **Expected proposer**: a delegate proved by coherent, context-pinned baking rights for that exact branch’s target level and round. Missing or conflicting rows mean unknown. This is distinct from an observed candidate’s producer, which also stays unknown without candidate-matching evidence. Knowing a proposer does not prove timing, and knowing timing does not prove a proposer. Sources: [FR003/083/086](../specs/observatory-foundations.md), [bakers](../src/rpc/networkData.ts), [opportunity capture](../src/consensus/opportunityFacts.ts).
- **Conditional deadline**: a protocol prediction justified by proven execution durations and a real reference timestamp/round. The next-level deadline is an earliest protocol boundary conditional on hypothetical production, not a promised actual timestamp. Successor durations apply to the predecessor-round term too. A same-protocol proven parent may justify a labelled hypothetical selected-round calculation without inventing a selected header. Unsupported migration basis makes the affected timer unavailable; unequal protocol hashes do not establish a predecessor-round reset. Sources: [FR087/088](../specs/observatory-foundations.md), [conditionalRoundBoundary and protocolRoundStart](../src/consensus/roundSchedule.ts), [pinned Octez source/applicability](./observatory-foundations-slice-9-impl.md#protocol-basis-and-applicability).
- **Captured opportunity**: the branch’s immutable original proposer/timing contexts and receipts, deadline, remaining duration at capture, wall capture date and monotonic capture time. Historical inspection renders those captured values; it does not substitute current rights/constants or continue counting down a historical remaining duration. No captured fact is fabricated for an uncaptured or evicted historical owner. Sources: [FR013/014/050/052/053/087](../specs/observatory-foundations.md), [captureOpportunityFacts](../src/consensus/opportunityFacts.ts), [captureOpportunities](../src/monitor/observationStore.ts).
- **Clock context**: an explicitly estimated current protocol round derived from an actually proved applied reference and target execution parameters, visibly independent of the selected branch deadlines. Its attribution names the original observer and actual parameter responder separately. A safe input does not guarantee a renderable computed output: nonfinite, unsafe or unrenderable calendar endpoints leave this estimate unavailable independently of valid branch facts. It is separate from the selected consensus and its two opportunities. Clock advance alone cannot expire coherent votes, select a different branch, refresh a raw receipt, retire identity authority or advance applied values. Sources: [FR028/049/055/082/085/087](../specs/observatory-foundations.md), [main](../src/main.ts), [S9 clock/parent-replacement regressions](./observatory-foundations-slice-9-impl.md).
- **Prediction elapsed / awaiting observation**: a justified deadline passed without the corresponding observed transition. The reader can report that conditional prediction state; it cannot manufacture a block, camera/history advance, event, global chain stall or finality. Missing prediction inputs remain unavailable/unknown. Sources: [FR022–024/088/089](../specs/observatory-foundations.md), [health and opportunity rendering](../src/main.ts), [dashboard](../src/dashboardView.ts).
- **Observed progression**: a source-attributed transition between actually observed heads: same-level round advancement, applied next-level advancement, same-height reorganization, or skipped delivered levels. Skips disclose an unobserved intermediate path; transport connectivity and progression remain independent. Sources: [FR022/024/089](../specs/observatory-foundations.md), [NetworkMonitor progression](../src/monitor/networkMonitor.ts), [AC15/34 production evidence](./observatory-foundations-slice-9-impl.md).

## Whole-review correction clarifications for controlled sync

- **Uncertain quorum display versus event**: reconstructed positive power, threshold, reached state, source and actual new as-of remain inspectable when bounded prior phase history is uncertain. They do not prove that an observed-quorum event was never emitted. No reconstructed first-ever event is appended under that uncertainty, and retained sibling events are neither cleared nor rewritten. Before history loss a genuine first quorum still emits its source-attributed observed-quorum event; prior-below crossing remains separately justified. Sources: [FR078/081/092 and C24](../specs/observatory-foundations.md), [phase/event owner](../src/monitor/observationStore.ts), [independent-finding correction](observatory-foundations-whole-retired-event-fix.md).
- **Source-local rendering without primary snapshot**: actual admitted B head/context/derived numerical facts and retained B inspection remain readable when primary A has no applied snapshot. No A snapshot, observation, history or receipt is synthesized. Primary-A applied values and its independent clock remain unavailable; actual B proof remains separately attributed. Sources: [FR025–028/AC3](../specs/observatory-foundations.md), [actual rendering](../src/main.ts), [whole-review correction](observatory-foundations-whole-autonomous-b-fix.md).
