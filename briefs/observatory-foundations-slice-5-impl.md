`src/monitor/observationStore.ts` admits that proof at the actual target boundary. A body completed before identity establishment cannot establish zero through later admission; a request begun earlier may establish zero when its completed-body receipt follows the exact identity and coherent same-source context is proven. Context changes, transport/body failure and lifecycle interruption withdraw current completeness without refreshing the old receipt. Coverage is compact: retained targets own `PendingCoverage` facts and normalized operation count, with no copied per-record operation-body arrays. ## Accepted phase state and event ownership

The observation store owns exact source/chain/full-context/level/round/payload/phase state and original phase evidence. Maxima retain their captured total, threshold, numerical basis and receipt, so later rights correction cannot relabel an earlier maximum. First authoritative observation already at quorum records observed quorum without inventing a below-to-threshold crossing. A crossing requires new accepted compatible vote evidence, unchanged numerical basis and a retained prior below-threshold state. Correction, replay, reconstruction, render, selection and re-admission do not reset known maxima or sticky quorum/crossing facts.

Events are immutable accepted facts with payload-shared attribution; votes shared by candidates do not invent candidate-specific signatures or duplicate crossings. Rendering consumes those facts instead of comparing the previous rendered selection with the next one. Per-network event retention is bounded at 12 independently of phase-state retention. Phase state is bounded at 500, with accepted products owning the relevant identities.

Loss of raw/product/phase history sets sticky crossing-history uncertainty. New or reconstructed identities after that loss can report observed quorum but cannot fabricate a fresh crossing; retained phase facts preserve their known maxima and crossing eligibility. This conservative limitation is visibly qualified as partial history. It prevents replay and a proven reorg return to a forgotten identity from recreating a crossing without an unbounded tombstone set. No clock-only or claimed-height retirement establishes historical certainty.

Batch processing gathers membership once per identity, binds retained target membership once and trims products before phase/event recomputation. This preserves shared bounds while avoiding repeated same-payload membership scans.
