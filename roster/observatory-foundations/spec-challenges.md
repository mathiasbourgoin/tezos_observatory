# Adversarial specification challenges — observatory-foundations

Fresh Step 4 review, 2026-10-14. Read the full validated intake, all eight stories and 24 scenarios, fresh research, clarifications and prior-art table. These are requirement challenges, not implementation proposals. The resolved 60-second freshness rule, immediate context invalidation, no comparison auto-refresh, observed block hash and conditional opportunity requirements are accepted inputs. ASTRA aesthetics remain outside this phase.

## Challenges

- **C-1 [US-1 S1/S2; US-8 S1]**: Two block identities share level/round/payload, while consensus operations identify payload rather than block hash. What precisely associates a vote with each candidate, and what candidate-specific threshold claim is justified when that association is unavailable? “Same precise candidate” cannot silently manufacture evidence absent from the operation.
- **C-2 [US-1 S2/S3]**: Is a delegate signing competing payloads counted once separately for each payload or rejected as contradictory evidence? The story prohibits combined payload power but does not define a conflicting delegate's per-payload receipt and power.
- **C-3 [US-1 S3]**: What is the rejection unit for a malformed rights row, contradictory duplicate, aggregate signer or threshold: the row, operation, full committee, or selected instance? Valid evidence must not disappear or remain apparently authoritative by an unspecified partial rejection policy.
- **C-4 [US-1 S3; US-8 S3]**: Individually safe integers can sum beyond the safe-integer boundary. What makes a total, cumulative power, signer count and derived deficit valid when every input passes its individual check? Are zero total/zero threshold admissible, and which timestamp formats and invalid dates are rejected?
- **C-5 [US-2 S1/S2]**: After a valid zero snapshot, a source disconnects or later delivers a partial batch. Does the old phase remain a timestamped measured zero, become stale/partial, or become unavailable? The required source-limited meaning is clear; the transition of the displayed phase state is not.
- **C-6 [US-2 S1]**: What proves that a pending response is a usable *full validated-pending snapshot* rather than an omitted field, truncated response, partial successful parse or snapshot spanning a head transition? The story requires completeness of a source snapshot without specifying its observable validation boundary.
- **C-7 [US-2 S3]**: What evidence/time rule distinguishes progressing, connected-without-progress, reconnecting-with-progress and unknown? Receipt age, head timestamp and transport state may disagree; no observable state transition boundary is specified.
- **C-8 [US-2 S3; US-3 S2]**: Does “immutable freshness information” mean immutable receipt/context facts with a changing age/state, or a permanently frozen freshness label? Historical data cannot be relabelled by providers, but elapsed time must not leave an old result falsely fresh.
- **C-9 [US-3 S1/S2]**: Precisely what context establishes current/current+1 across a fork: network identity, source, head hash, predecessor, protocol, target level, round and payload? What happens when the same source changes its validated head while previously accepted target evidence remains inspectable?
- **C-10 [US-3 S3]**: “At most one attempt per 15 seconds for a new material context” does not define material equality or whether the rate limit is per source or per distinct context. Does rapidly changing context permit multiple starts within 15 seconds, and may a failed unchanged context ever be retried on a later explicit observation?
- **C-11 [US-3 S3; US-4 S3]**: One workflow per network/source does not bound the number of sources, queued contexts, response size or unresolved operations within a candidate. What observable resource bounds prevent a flood of source identities or same-candidate operations from bypassing the 100-candidate quarantine?
- **C-12 [US-4 S2/S3]**: How do manual pause, hidden visibility and pagehide combine? After a user pauses, may visibility restoration resume collection? After pagehide followed by back/forward-cache restoration, what permits a fresh generation and what still prevents external work?
- **C-13 [US-4 S2]**: Are aliases and cached auxiliary data allowed to update the visible inspector after cancellation without a network response committing? The story names cancellation but does not distinguish locally available permitted updates from forbidden obsolete-generation commits.
- **C-14 [US-5 S1; US-6 S2]**: “Pin ... evidence ... remain stable” conflicts with late evidence for the pinned identity and aliases arriving during search. Is pinned evidence an immutable snapshot, or may same-identity facts update while inspection state stays stable? Which receipt/power/eligibility/alias fields can change?
- **C-15 [US-5 S2]**: What uniquely identifies a full session instance versus active accepted candidate versus unresolved candidate, including multiple observers and shared payload? Does promotion remove the unresolved entry, and can the same identity consume several retention slots across categories?
- **C-16 [US-5 S2]**: Which receipt controls oldest eviction: first observation, latest replay, latest genuine new evidence, or promotion receipt? How are equal receipts ordered? Replays must not duplicate, but unspecified receipt refresh can let repeated evidence displace newer distinct identities.
- **C-17 [US-5 S1/S3]**: What does “newer-items count” count, when does it reset, and what does its action select? What happens when follow-live finds no coherent candidate, or when an evicted frozen pin is replaced by another pin? The single frozen-snapshot bound alone does not define these user-visible outcomes.
- **C-18 [US-6 S1/S2]**: What constitutes an alias/address search match: substring versus exact, case sensitivity, empty input and missing/changing alias? Which explicit sort keys and tie ordering must remain stable? A test cannot assert search or ordering without these semantics.
- **C-19 [US-6 S1/S3]**: Does the searchable committee include received delegates, absent delegates, unresolved signer identities and delegates excluded by invalid rights? What phase-specific receipt states distinguish unavailable coverage, observed absence in a snapshot and later received evidence?
- **C-20 [US-7 S1/S2]**: What constitutes agreement versus divergence versus partial when B is on a later level, shares payload but has a different block hash, or cannot prove selected historical A context? A fetched current B head must not become an unsupported comparison of the selected historical instance.
- **C-21 [US-7 S2]**: Which endpoint reads count toward three, including network identity checks, rights reuse validation, redirects and incomplete bodies? Are eight seconds measured from each request start through body parsing, and is a workflow allowed to last 24 seconds? What occurs on an explicit second click during the first workflow?
- **C-22 [US-7 S3]**: Does the exact 60-second boundary remain fresh at age 60 seconds or stale at that instant? Which clock defines elapsed age if wall time changes? If selected context changes away and back, can the old attributed result regain freshness before its expiry, or does invalidation remain permanent?
- **C-23 [US-8 S1]**: Is a threshold event distinct per phase, and does a first accepted observation already at quorum create an event without a prior observed below-threshold state? Can later rights correction or an observed reduction followed by recovery emit another crossing for the same identity?
- **C-24 [US-8 S1/S2]**: Events must retain history, but no event retention bound or relationship to evicted session history is declared. Which identity facts survive eviction, and can repeated render/resume/reconciliation of the same accepted observation duplicate an event?
- **C-25 [US-8 S3]**: Which explicit conditions choose the same-level next-round and next-level round-zero opportunity branches, and can both be shown simultaneously? What evidence establishes the expected proposer when coherent RPC baking rights have missing rounds or contradictory rows? The observed block hash is mandatory; a future hash remains unavailable until observed.
- **C-26 [US-8 S3]**: What protocol inputs, reference timestamp and clock assumptions justify the prediction countdown, and what is shown when the predicted time passes without an observed block? What exact observed change qualifies as same-level round progression versus applied next-level advancement across a reorg or skipped delivered heads?
- **C-27 [US-7; US-3]**: Prior art **Explorus prioritized backup cascade** does sequential provider fallback; stories assume an explicit independent comparison without cascade—justify divergence/adopt Y. Separately, the prior-art table mentions retaining contextual primary fallback: which primary paths may still cascade, and how are their source identities preserved?
- **C-28 [US-2; US-6; US-8]**: Prior art **Explorus quorum/network-health and missing/finalized labels** does broader health/missing/finality presentation; stories assume source-limited evidence and exclude unverified finality/network-wide inactivity—justify divergence/adopt Y. Which evidence-supported labels remain allowed so users do not infer the excluded claim from an ambiguous “missing” or “healthy” state?
- **C-29 [US-5; US-7]**: Prior art **Teztale archived multi-observer history and observer-difference timelines** does archived multi-observer inspection; stories assume bounded browser-session history, explicit single-provider comparison and deferred receipt timelines—justify divergence/adopt Y. What session boundary and restart behavior makes loss of historical evidence explicit to a reader? This prior-art claim derives from the supplied pinned-source local review, not a fresh successful GitLab fetch.

## Edge Cases

- **EC-1 [US-1]**: Same payload P in candidates H1/H2, one payload-only vote — distinct candidates and no invented producer are required; attribution of vote power to each candidate is unspecified (C-1).
- **EC-2 [US-1]**: An aggregate repeats a signer internally and arrives before its individual vote — unique delegate power is required; a partly invalid aggregate's rejection scope is unspecified (C-3).
- **EC-3 [US-2]**: A full coherent zero snapshot predates instance establishment by one millisecond — it cannot establish measured zero. A newer coherent post-establishment snapshot can establish source-snapshot zero.
- **EC-4 [US-2]**: Stream connected, no new head; another observer reports head advancement — source health and chain progress remain independent; transition timing is unspecified (C-7).
- **EC-5 [US-3]**: Same-height H1→H2 reorg while H1 eligibility is age one second — invalidated H1 result is stale immediately and cannot populate H2; prior accepted H1 inspection disposition is unspecified (C-9).
- **EC-6 [US-3]**: Global A head 120, B coherent head 122, evidence level 123 — count only with B coherent rights/identity; do not reject solely by A's head or a clock ceiling.
- **EC-7 [US-4]**: Hidden initialization followed by manual pause and visibility restoration — initially no external requests; whether restoration can resume despite manual pause is unspecified (C-12).
- **EC-8 [US-4]**: Aborted fetch resolves after resume with identical head — cancelled generation cannot commit, including alias/comparison responses.
- **EC-9 [US-5]**: All categories overflow at equal receipt timestamps, including pinned history — bounds stay independent and one frozen pin survives; deterministic tie ordering is unspecified (C-16).
- **EC-10 [US-5]**: Follow-live invoked while newest observations remain unresolved — no fabricated coherent selection is allowed; concrete selection/result is unspecified (C-17).
- **EC-11 [US-6]**: New alias makes a previously unmatched row match while search is focused — focus/scroll/inspection target must remain stable; row inclusion/order timing is unspecified (C-18).
- **EC-12 [US-6]**: Missing rights but one observed signer and empty other phase — no complete missing set or global inactivity label; searchable unresolved signer treatment is unspecified (C-19).
- **EC-13 [US-7]**: B wrong network returns a syntactically valid committee — no counted comparison power; raw observations remain unknown-power or unavailable.
- **EC-14 [US-7]**: Comparison ages beyond 60 seconds without selection change — stale with receipt time, no automatic refresh. Exact boundary and clock behavior are unspecified (C-22).
- **EC-15 [US-8]**: First accepted batch already has threshold power — exact power is required; crossing-event eligibility without an observed prior lower state is unspecified (C-23).
- **EC-16 [US-8]**: Same-level later round observed while no next-level block arrives — distinguish round progression from applied next-level advancement; observed block hash is shown and future block hash remains unavailable.
- **EC-17 [US-8]**: Either conditional opportunity lacks coherent baking rights or protocol timing — expected proposer/countdown must expose unavailable rather than invented values; branch trigger and expired-prediction state are unspecified (C-25/C-26).
