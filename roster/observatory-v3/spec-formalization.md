# Observatory V3 normative requirements and acceptance criteria

Controlled drafting input; not implementation or gate evidence. All 28 challenge resolutions, including formerly pending audit rows, use the explicitly approved addendum and intake policies 1–9. Local IDs are V3 requirements; qualified dependency IDs identify the current foundations contract. Normative Entities remain those in specs/observatory-foundations.md.

## Functional requirements

### US-1

- **FR-001** [US-1]: When an investigator selects evidence, the observatory MUST expose its exact network, source, context, level, round and payload identity separately from applied-head identity. Scenario trace: [US-1-S1; US-1-S2].
- **FR-002** [US-1]: When an investigator selects votes-only evidence, the observatory MUST label it votes-only and show proposal hash and producer unavailable rather than invent either. Scenario trace: [US-1-S1].
- **FR-003** [US-1]: When an investigator selects competing candidates, the observatory MUST expose each actual candidate hash with producer attribution only from matching candidate evidence. Scenario trace: [US-1-S2].
- **FR-004** [US-1]: When candidates share a payload, the observatory MUST label the shared voting evidence without doubling its power or treating it as candidate-specific signatures; unrelated payload evidence remains independent. Scenario trace: [US-1-S2].
- **FR-005** [US-1]: When an investigator reads either phase, the observatory MUST expose exact power, committee denominator, threshold, deficit and signer counts with that phase’s source/context/receipt/age and coverage qualification. Scenario trace: [US-1-S1; US-1-S3].
- **FR-006** [US-1]: When phase evidence lacks complete coherent established-identity source coverage, the observatory MUST distinguish partial/unavailable from justified measured zero independently for each phase. Scenario trace: [US-1-S3].
- **FR-007** [US-1]: When an observer disconnects, the observatory MUST describe observer/source health without asserting a global chain stall. Scenario trace: [US-1-S3].
- **FR-008** [US-1]: When evidence is selected or rendered, the observatory MUST NOT create a threshold crossing: first at-quorum evidence remains observed-quorum, and a crossing requires prior below-threshold evidence for the identical phase/identity/context and remains at most once through replay, eviction, retirement and reorg-return. Scenario trace: [US-1-S2].

### US-2

- **FR-009** [US-2]: When a reader follows live, the observatory MUST present previous verified block LEFT, selected actual N/R centrally, conditional N/R+1 UP and conditional N+1/R0 RIGHT as a branching spatial navigator rather than a one-dimensional list. Scenario trace: [US-2-S1].
- **FR-010** [US-2]: When a coherent adjacent actual same-level round advance is accepted while following live, the observatory MUST move the observed camera UP to that actual destination. Scenario trace: [US-2-S1].
- **FR-011** [US-2]: When a coherent adjacent actual next-level advance is accepted while following live, the observatory MUST move the observed camera RIGHT to that actual destination. Scenario trace: [US-2-S1].
- **FR-012** [US-2]: When an eligible adjacent progress transition animates, the observatory MUST use a default duration of 160ms and a maximum duration of 200ms. Scenario trace: [US-2-S1; US-2-S4].
- **FR-013** [US-2]: When further updates arrive before a transition finishes, the observatory MUST coalesce to the latest eligible accepted destination with at most one transition in flight, no reenactment queue, and settlement within 200ms after the final accepted update; a burst ending at the next level uses one RIGHT transition. Scenario trace: [US-2-S4].
- **FR-014** [US-2]: When accepted progress skips observations or has unknown continuity, the observatory MUST recenter directly/static with an explicit gap and no invented intermediate node or progression animation. Scenario trace: [US-2-S3].
- **FR-015** [US-2]: When context reorgs, source changes or a same-coordinate candidate changes, the observatory MUST replace/recenter identity statically with explicit status rather than animate progression; a gap/reorg/unknown final destination cancels in-flight progress motion. Scenario trace: [US-2-S3].
- **FR-016** [US-2]: When showing relationships, the observatory MUST distinguish context-proven same-level round continuity from verified actual block ancestry; coordinates or payload equality alone cannot establish solid block ancestry. Scenario trace: [US-2-S1; US-2-S3].
- **FR-017** [US-2]: When clock estimates r6→7, a prediction expires, duplicate receipts arrive or numerical vote facts change while observed successor r0 is valid, the observatory MUST keep observed anchor/camera/history at r0 and create no observed rounds 1–6; later predicted clock context remains separate. Scenario trace: [US-2-S2].
- **FR-018** [US-2]: When a successor r0’s parent/context is invalidated, the observatory MUST withdraw its live/numerical authority and retain it only as attributed historical/unresolved evidence until coherence is proven. Scenario trace: [US-2-S3].
- **FR-019** [US-2]: When a frozen pin’s context becomes invalid, the observatory MUST retain its historical/as-of captured facts without presenting them as current LIVE authority; any separate current status remains qualified. Scenario trace: [US-2-S3].
- **FR-020** [US-2]: When displaying retained/collapsed history or gaps, the observatory MUST describe only actually retained observations and session receipt limits without implying unseen elapsed observations, canonicality or finality. Scenario trace: [US-2-S3; US-2-S4].

- **FR-077** [US-2]: When a coherent adjacent next-level observation resets to round 0 while following live, the observatory MUST move camera focus horizontally RIGHT with vertical focus unchanged; a same-level round advance moves vertically UP with horizontal focus unchanged, including applied/observed high-round contexts. Scenario trace: [US-2-S5].
- **FR-078** [US-2]: When displaying large numerical levels/rounds or bounded retained history, the observatory MUST preserve usable bounded navigator geometry with compact honest gaps/collapsed retained counts rather than producing huge unbounded offscreen coordinates. Scenario trace: [US-2-S5].

### US-3

- **FR-021** [US-3]: When a reader inspects selected N/R, the observatory MUST show both exact conditional targets N/R+1 and N+1/R0 with separate proposer, timer and status fields, including when one or both are unavailable. Scenario trace: [US-3-S1].
- **FR-022** [US-3]: When a reader views any supported viewport, the observatory MUST keep current and both future options together in one intact readable overview reachable by ordinary vertical scroll, without local pan, dragging or disclosure for critical target/proposer/timer/status. Scenario trace: [US-3-S1].
- **FR-023** [US-3]: When rights justify a future proposer, the observatory MUST attribute it to coherent RPC source/context evidence and show the future hash unavailable; conditional predictions remain visibly distinct from actual observations. Scenario trace: [US-3-S1].
- **FR-024** [US-3]: When displaying a timer, the observatory MUST qualify it by applicable coherent protocol constants, reference context, rights and timing assumptions, including hypothetical predecessor production for next-level estimates; fixture deadlines and six-second values are not protocol constants. Scenario trace: [US-3-S1; US-3-S2; US-3-S3].
- **FR-025** [US-3]: When either branch lacks justified rights or timing, the observatory MUST mark that branch independently unavailable without borrowing the other branch’s proposer/deadline. Scenario trace: [US-3-S2].
- **FR-026** [US-3]: When a conditional timer expires, the observatory MUST show elapsed/awaiting observation while keeping the selected-relative upper target at R+1 rather than replacing it with a later clock round. Scenario trace: [US-3-S3].
- **FR-027** [US-3]: When a reader pins current evidence or selects a historical capture, the observatory MUST preserve captured deadline and remaining duration with actual capture/observation time, source/context and historical assumptions, without current-rights or invented pin-time recalculation. Scenario trace: [US-3-S3].
- **FR-028** [US-3]: When a historical branch lacks captured rights/timing, the observatory MUST keep it independently unavailable rather than fill it from later/current context. Scenario trace: [US-3-S2; US-3-S3].
- **FR-029** [US-3]: When a reader resumes Follow live, the observatory MUST restore current coherent timers independently for both branches. Scenario trace: [US-3-S3].

Scenario trace: [US-4-S1; US-4-S2; US-4-S3].
- **FR-032** [US-4]: When a reader invokes Latest observed, the observatory MUST center and pin the newest coherent LIVE-eligible selectable identity by first accepted receipt as a one-off action; no qualifying live item yields explicit unavailable rather than historical substitution. Scenario trace: [US-4-S2].
- **FR-033** [US-4]: When a reader invokes Follow live, the observatory MUST enable ongoing eligible selection/camera following, or show explicit live unavailable without substituting historical evidence. Scenario trace: [US-4-S2].
- **FR-034** [US-4]: When a reader invokes Previous observation, the observatory MUST traverse distinct actually retained selected-network selectable identities by first accepted monotonic receipt, including candidate siblings; oldest and frozen-outside-history selections show Previous unavailable, and receipt navigation does not claim ancestry. Scenario trace: [US-4-S3].
- **FR-035** [US-4]: When arrivals follow a pin baseline, the observatory MUST count only first accepted distinct selectable identities as newer: replay/enrichment do not increment, candidate-free promotion preserves its unit/receipt without increment, distinct sibling increments, and new same-receipt identities use stable lexical ties. Scenario trace: [US-4-S1].
- **FR-036** [US-4]: When collection updates a retained pin, the observatory MUST preserve selected identity, camera, keyboard focus, document/local scroll, query, reader ordering and disclosure state. Scenario trace: [US-4-S1].
- **FR-037** [US-4]: When accepted same-identity facts enrich a retained pin, the observatory MUST allow the attributed vote enrichment while preserving captured opportunities unchanged. Scenario trace: [US-4-S1].
- **FR-038** [US-4]: When an active candidate leaves the active index while its history instance remains retained, the observatory MUST keep historical candidate inspection available rather than prematurely freeze/remove it; each snapshot retains at most 100 candidate facts with explicit partial retention and pinned fact protection. Scenario trace: [US-4-S2].
- **FR-039** [US-4]: When the pinned history entry itself is evicted beyond the 150-instance session bound, the observatory MUST preserve one entirely static frozen snapshot per network labelled outside session history, replacing it when the pin changes; accepted/unresolved candidate bounds remain 100/100. Scenario trace: [US-4-S2].
- **FR-040** [US-4]: When manual pause, hidden document or pagehide disallows collection, the observatory MUST start no external work from inspection, navigation, theme or effects, cancel obsolete work and prevent obsolete responses committing after resume; manual pause and inspection mode survive independently. Scenario trace: [US-4-S4].
- **FR-041** [US-4]: When cancelled comparison has no committed result, the observatory MUST show cancelled/unavailable; any prior committed result stays attributed stale, and context invalidation remains stale even after switching back before 60 seconds, without automatic refresh. Scenario trace: [US-4-S4].
- **FR-042** [US-4]: When retaining/replaying evidence, the observatory MUST preserve mandatory attribution while honoring the accepted 10000-operation and unique delegate/identity provenance bounds; truncation cannot certify complete coverage or zero. Scenario trace: [US-4-S1; US-4-S2].

### US-5

- **FR-043** [US-5]: When a reader searches committee rows, the observatory MUST trim the query and match alias/address case-insensitive substrings; empty query restores all matching eligible rows. Scenario trace: [US-5-S1].
- **FR-044** [US-5]: When a reader explicitly sorts by alias, address or power, the observatory MUST order by that criterion with address ties; subsequent alias/evidence arrivals cannot automatically resort existing matching rows, and new matches append. Scenario trace: [US-5-S1].
- **FR-045** [US-5]: When alias correction removes a query match, the observatory MUST hide nonfocused rows but retain a focused row marked pending search exclusion until focus leaves, preserving focus and existing matching order. Scenario trace: [US-5-S1].
- **FR-046** [US-5]: When a reader inspects committee evidence, the observatory MUST expose exact phase-specific receipt, power, share, eligibility and source/context, distinguishing source-qualified absent/zero from partial/unavailable rather than network-wide inactivity. Scenario trace: [US-5-S1; US-5-S2].
- **FR-047** [US-5]: When a signer lacks proven rights or power, the observatory MUST keep the observation separately inspectable as unranked raw evidence rather than a validated committee or missing-power claim. Scenario trace: [US-5-S2].
- **FR-048** [US-5]: When a reader explicitly compares a second source, the observatory MUST expose exact actually compared fields, contexts, fetched-at age and divergence/partial/unavailable states without pooling powers, relabelling cached rights provenance or claiming unproven fields. Scenario trace: [US-5-S3].
- **FR-049** [US-5]: When an explicit comparison runs, the observatory MUST limit it to one in-flight workflow, at most three reads, at most 8 seconds per read and 24 seconds overall, with no retry, fourth read, cascade or periodic refresh. Scenario trace: [US-5-S3].
- **FR-050** [US-5]: When selecting comparison reads, the observatory MUST prioritize identity then head then pending; an alternative third read requires pending proven unavailable within the same workflow and budget, and incompatible cached rights leave power unknown. Scenario trace: [US-5-S3].
- **FR-051** [US-5]: When comparison age reaches 60 seconds or its context is invalidated, the observatory MUST label it stale without silently fetching again. Scenario trace: [US-5-S3].
