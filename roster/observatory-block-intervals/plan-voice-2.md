## 3. Identified risks

- **Mutable integration target:** independently active V3 work can invalidate lifecycle and reader assumptions after a local feature pass. Review/QA evidence must follow the final integrated revision.
- **Deadline scheduling:** the serial initial E and final recheck surround floor/fallback work. Six reads with two concurrency slots cannot all consume their full individual 8-second allowance and necessarily fit in 24 seconds. The 8 seconds is an upper bound, not a promised duration for every read; the batch deadline must terminate remaining work without publishing a subset.
- **False timeout safety:** abort alone does not settle a noncooperating fetch/body promise. A deadline that merely signals abort can leave state active or admit late commits; explicit settlement plus generation fencing needs adversarial verification.
- **Boundary and error conflation:** an empty floor, invalid floor and valid unusable pair have different outcomes. A generic catch/fallback path can accidentally invent partial history or hide a long stall.
- **False freshness:** a fresh fetch of old E, synchronization metadata and completion age are different facts. Deriving lag from temporally filtered E or using wall-clock deltas would mislead readers even with a correct mean.
- **Atomicity leaks:** per-window completion could update the view before final consistency or whole-batch deadline success. Mixing old and new windows would produce apparently coherent values with different T.
- **Lifecycle event collisions:** hidden/pagehide, BFCache resume, rollback and queued head changes can race within one network. Network selection must remain presentation-only, and the other runtime must retain independent limits/results.
- **Unknown provider facts:** endpoint agreement is not intermediate-history or ancestry proof. Unsupported headers or a provider smoke cannot strengthen the claim beyond the explicitly qualified indexed span.
- **Presentation breadth:** three sets of evidence and long failure/details text can crowd the existing overview on short phones or large zoom. A visually compact happy path does not demonstrate preservation across failure and reader states.
