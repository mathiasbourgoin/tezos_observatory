# Observatory mark

The header combines the classic Tezos mark with a telescope in a forest-green circle. The telescope objective and stepped eyepiece were refined after reviewing rendered alternatives. The favicon uses the same silhouette with fewer small details.

Both assets are self-contained SVG paths without fonts or external resources. The palette is forest `#064B40`, ivory `#F5F0DE` and sage `#92C5AA`. Review included actual 16, 32, 40 and 64 pixel renders on light and dark backgrounds, followed by integration screenshots at desktop and mobile widths. At 16 pixels, the silhouette takes priority over telescope detail.

The classic Tezos geometry was retrieved from the [CDNLogo SVG mirror](https://static.cdnlogo.com/logos/t/33/tezos.svg). [Wikimedia Commons attribution](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tezos_logo.svg) identifies tezos.com as its source, Arthur Breitman as its author and CC0 as its license. The mirror is the retrieval source; it was not downloaded from the official brand package. Telescope geometry is newly drawn, informed by the maintainer's circular telescope reference.

Production assets: `src/public/observatory-mark.svg` for the header and `src/public/favicon.svg` for the favicon.
